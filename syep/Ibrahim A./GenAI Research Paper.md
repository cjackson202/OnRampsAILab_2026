Generative AI, also known as GenAI, is a form of artificial intelligence that generates original content in response to a prompt made by a user. The requests can stem from a variety of user needs such as assistance, suggestions, or ideas. Generative AI is powered by machine learning models that are trained on large amounts of data to identify patterns, language, and relationships. Instead of simply leveraging stored information, the model analyzes the input, interprets the context, and generates the proper response based on the patterns learned from training. Overall, Generative AI is one of many technological advancements in society by allowing machines to produce original content that supports creativity and solves problems across different industries. 

Microsoft Copilot is an AI assistant that was developed by Microsoft to complete tasks faster, automate repetitive tasks, and deliver suggestions and ideas to make better decisions. Microsoft advertises that Copilot can also be used to create documents, analyze data, manage projects, and effectively communicate. The products that Microsoft Copilot offers derive from Generative AI. For instance, a user can input anything into the prompt and the machine learning model would take this prompt, understand its context, predict what outcome follows it through pattern recognition, and then generate the response. Copilot is used by many businesses and organizations that rely on artificial intelligence to boost productivity, innovate, and solve problems.

AI Agents are a subset of artificial intelligence that involves an autonomous system that utilizes available tools to perform a wide-range of tasks and solve certain problems. Using Large Language Models (LLMs), AI Agents are able to understand the needs of the user and solve the task accordingly. When the task becomes too complex for the agent to solve, it is able to harness external tools. One example is Microsoft Copilot Studio, which is designed to allow businesses to develop, test, and publish AI agents. Additionally, businesses use this application to efficiently solve complex tasks such as software design, code generation, IT automation, and assistance through conversation. Ultimately, AI agents allow for efficient operations within a business, making complex tasks more simple to complete. 

Generative AI has numerous real-world influences in many industries. For instance, in healthcare, it is used to integrate and analyze medical data, and assist healthcare professionals in delivering more efficient and personalized methods of patient care. According to McKinsey & Company, the technology is able to sum up large amounts of patient data records, which allows providers to attend to more intricate patient needs. Similarly, companies such as Accenture use Microsoft Copilot to automate tasks and processes. For instance, employees use Copilot to draft documents, analyze data in Excel, summarize emails, and design slide decks. Generative AI allows professionals to make their work more efficient, while focusing their time on higher prioritized and valued responsibilities.
