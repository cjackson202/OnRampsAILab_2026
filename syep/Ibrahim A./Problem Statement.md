Trip planning becomes overwhelming for wheelchair users because they must navigate multiple websites to determine whether destinations, attractions, hotels, transportation services, and other travel accommodations are accessible. In addition to comparing destinations, and estimating trip expenses, travelers with mobility impairments spend a significant amount of time searching for accessibility information, such as wheelchair-accessible entrances, elevators, restrooms, and public-transportation options. This process is time-consuming as accessibility information is scattered across different websites and is frequently inconsistent and incomplete. The organizational burden creates a disconnect between trip planning and enjoying the travel experience. Ultimately, there is a demand for a solution that simplifies the process of trip planning and improves access to reliable accessibility information for wheelchair users.

An AI accessible travel assistant can address the issue by providing travelers with a mobility impairment travelers with an integrated and centralized platform for planning accessible domestic travel experiences. When a user enters details such as their budget, accessibility needs, travel dates, destination preferences, interests, and number of travelers, the assistant can generate a personalized itinerary that prioritizes accessibility. In addition to offering information about flights, weather, attractions, lodging, and transportation, the assistant can identify accessibility features such as wheelchair-accessible entrances, elevators, restrooms, accessible hotel rooms, and public transportation accommodations. By consolidating reliable accessibility information from multiple sources into a single conversational experience, the AI assistant reduces the time and effort required to research accessible travel options. As a result, travelers can make informed decisions, plan with greater confidence, and focus more on enjoying their travel experience instead of enduring challenges involving accessibility navigation.
